# Amaltas Villa by Jungle Diaries — Brand brief

Taken from the landing page (amaltasvilla.com), November 2026. Nothing in this folder is used by the website build.

## Positioning

A boutique villa on Dhela Road, Jim Corbett, built for a slower, more personal Corbett: a handful of keys, a forest for a neighbour, and hospitality that hosts guests rather than processes them. Days have no fixed shape: breakfast among the greenery, an afternoon with a book on the terrace, an evening by the fire.

- **Hero:** "Leave the city behind. Let the forest set the pace."
- **About headline:** "Not Every Getaway Needs An Itinerary."
- **Ticker:** Forest Views · Slow Living · Bonfire Nights · Jungle-Bound · Private Pool
- **Audience:** "Couples find quiet. Families find room to breathe. Small groups find a place that feels like their own."
- **Closing line:** "An Intimate Forest Escape At The Heart Of The Corbett Experience"
